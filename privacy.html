Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · DRAFT, not yet in effect

This policy explains what the Honestech Mobile Mechanic App collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and an encrypted password.
  • Business data you enter: your business details, jobs, inspections, customers and their vehicles, estimates, invoices, expenses, mileage and settings.
  • Photos, videos and receipts you add. These are currently stored on your device only.
  • Location, only when you use features like "On my way" or route planning, to estimate drive times. It isn't stored on our servers.
  • Payment info is handled by Stripe. We receive your plan status, not your card number.

How we use it

Only to run the App for you: saving and syncing your data across your devices, creating your documents, and managing your subscription. We don't sell your data or your customers' data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase: cloud database and sign-in.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the App.
  • OpenStreetMap services: address lookups and drive distances. Addresses are sent to them to look up.
  • NHTSA: VIN lookups. The VIN is sent to them.

Where it's stored

Data is kept on your device, and in the cloud if you sign in. Each business's cloud data is separated and only accessible to that business's signed-in members.

Your choices

You can export a backup at any time, sign out, or ask us to delete your account and cloud data. Data saved on your device can be removed by deleting the App's website data.

Children

The App is for businesses and isn't meant for children under 13.
